01 — Overview

What Robin Insights actually is.

A BU-level people-analytics cockpit for HRBPs, HR Head and CHRO — headcount, compensation, leave, attendance, performance, engagement and exits, in one screen. Built on a full role-based access and audit layer, because this is HR's most sensitive data.

02 — Problem

HRBPs fly into every business review blind.

Headcount lives in Darwinbox. Engagement lives in a survey tool. Performance lives in a third system. Attrition risk lives in nobody's system — it's a gut call.

Before every BU review: export, reconcile, re-type. By hand. Every single cycle.

The workaround today

Manual exports, stitched into a spreadsheet, then licensed Power BI seats just to look at them — paid infrastructure for a view that's still hours behind the data.

Result: insights that arrive late, disagree with each other across systems, rarely go deeper than a headcount number — and come with a recurring bill.

03 — Solution

One cockpit. Every BU, one screen, always current.

Robin Insights pulls headcount, attrition risk, performance, engagement and exits into one dashboard per business unit — and writes the paragraph an HRBP would otherwise have typed by hand.

Live metrics

Six headline numbers per BU, with month-on-month movement — not a snapshot.

AI narrative

"Attrition in Sales is up 12% MoM, driven by X" — written for the reader, not left for them to infer from a chart.

Risk & heat maps

Who's likely to leave, why, and how every business unit compares side by side.

HR calendar

Appraisals, confirmations and work anniversaries, 30 days out.

Export

The business review deck as a PDF in under a second, or the filtered employee list as CSV — PII columns included only for a caller cleared to see them.

Drill-down

By grade, location, tenure and period — the whole page moves together.

Same numbers — however many times, however many people, open it — and no Power BI seat to license for it.

Access control, not an afterthought

Every read is scoped to business unit and permission, server-side — denied with a 403, never a quiet empty page — and logged to an audit trail.

Narrative — grounded, not guessed

Every figure the model writes is checked against the fact sheet it was given. An unverifiable number is rewritten once, then replaced with a computed summary. Never invented.

Ask Robin — the assistant

A chat panel briefed only on the metrics the signed-in reader is already cleared to see. No database access of its own — it cannot answer with anything it wasn't handed.

What it integrates with

Seven HR Ops extracts ingested today. A generic polled-API connector — endpoint, interval, JSON path, configured on screen — is the same door a live Darwinbox or survey feed walks through. No new code to point it there.
